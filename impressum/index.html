Basic direction (Grundlegende Richtung)

Reports on the cleanups Sapu Earth pays for in Indonesia and the Philippines, with a public receipt for each, and stories with numbers and comment on local waste collection and on how the responsible authorities answer.

Berichte über die von Sapu Earth bezahlten Aufräumeinsätze in Indonesien und auf den Philippinen, mit einem öffentlichen Beleg für jeden Einsatz, sowie Geschichten mit Zahlen und Kommentaren zur örtlichen Müllabfuhr und dazu, wie die zuständigen Behörden antworten.

Report illegal content

Think something on sapu.earth is illegal? Email hello@sapu.earth with the link to it and why you think it is illegal, and say that what you write is true as far as you know. You may add your name, but you do not have to. We confirm that your report arrived and tell you what we decided. How we handle your email is in the privacy notice below. This address is also our contact point for authorities and users under the EU Digital Services Act. Crews can also write to us in their WhatsApp chat with Sapu Earth. We answer in English or German.

What this site is

Sapu Earth is a private, self-funded project. It pays local cleanup crews and publishes every cleanup as a public receipt: photos and videos, kilograms, the crew and the money paid. Under STORIES it publishes reports about trash and the cleanups, with every source linked.

Replies and corrections

Is something in a story wrong, or do you want to reply to it? Email hello@sapu.earth, in Indonesian, English or German. We correct mistakes and date each correction, and we publish replies from the people and offices we write about.

PRIVACY FOR VISITORS

What happens to your data when you visit sapu.earth. Crews who apply or upload get their own notice on those pages, linked above.

  • Who: Sapu Earth is run by Marcus Pousette in Innsbruck, Austria. Contact: hello@sapu.earth.
  • Hosting: Cloudflare hosts this site for us, as our processor. To send you a page, Cloudflare's servers receive your internet address (IP address) and what your browser sends with each request, such as the address of the page. No website can be shown without this.
  • Flood protection: Cloudflare counts the requests from each internet address. An address that sends too many in a short time is briefly blocked. This keeps the site working for everyone.
  • What we keep: nothing about your visit. Thanking a crew under a receipt only adds one to a count and says nothing about you (Thanks, below). Our own code and database never store your internet address. Only the crew application form keeps a scrambled code made from it, for about a day, to stop spam. This site sets no cookies. All its files, fonts and the globe included, come from sapu.earth, and the pages load nothing from other companies, except satellite pictures when you zoom in far on the globe (below).
  • Satellite map: when you zoom in far on the globe on the homepage, or tap SEE THE SPOT ON SATELLITE, your browser loads satellite pictures straight from EOX IT Services GmbH in Vienna, Austria. EOX then receives your internet address and the address of our site, as with any picture loaded from a website. Nothing loads from EOX before you zoom in that far, and we send EOX nothing else. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in showing you the places you zoom in to (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)); satellite pictures of the whole planet cannot reasonably come from our own servers. EOX's own notice: eox.at/privacy-notice.
  • Thanks: if you tap SAY THANK YOU under a receipt (SAY TERIMA KASIH on a receipt from Indonesia or Malaysia), our code uses only which receipt it was, after checking that the tap came from a page of sapu.earth. As with any page, Cloudflare receives the request (Hosting, above). Our database adds one to that receipt's count and to the counts of that day (UTC), which keep the thanks within daily limits. It keeps nothing about you: no internet address, no time of day, no cookie, and nothing saved on your device. The page remembers your tap only while it is open, and the counts cannot show who thanked. The counts show on the receipt, the crew's page and the list of crews, and go when the receipt goes off sapu.earth. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in letting visitors thank the crews (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Instagram and TikTok: a receipt may link to a post about it on Instagram or TikTok, ours or the crew's own, where people can comment, and a crew's page and the list of crews may link to the crew's own TikTok account. Each is a plain link: nothing loads from Instagram or TikTok until you tap it, and we never show their posts, comments or counts on sapu.earth. After the tap you are on their site or in their app, where their own privacy notices apply. These links do not tell them which of our pages you came from.
  • Shown in a photo or video? if you appear in a photo or video on this site and want it taken down, email hello@sapu.earth. We then stop using it and remove it where we can. How we use the photos and videos crews send is in the notice for crew uploads.
  • Emails and reports: if you email hello@sapu.earth, for example to report something illegal, we use your email address, your name if you give it, and what you write only to handle it and to answer you. If we take something down after your report, we tell the crew why, but not who reported it. Emails reach us through Cloudflare (its Email Routing) and are kept in our mailbox at Google. We delete them at the latest one year after the matter is closed. A report a crew sends in its WhatsApp chat with us is used the same way, only to handle it and to answer, and we delete it from that chat at the latest one year after the matter is closed. Legal basis: for a report, our legal duty to handle it (GDPR Art. 6(1)(c), with Art. 16 of the EU Digital Services Act); otherwise our legitimate interest in answering you (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Why: to show you the site and to keep it safe and working. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in a safe, working website (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Where: on Cloudflare servers, which may be outside the EU. Cloudflare is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and uses the EU standard contractual clauses for other countries. Our mailbox is with Google, which may also use servers outside the EU and is certified under the same framework.
  • Your rights: ask to see, correct or delete your details, or object to this processing, by emailing hello@sapu.earth. You can also complain to the Austrian data protection authority (dsb.gv.at).
