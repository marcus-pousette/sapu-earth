# Security

Found a way to see data that should be private, to act as a crew or as the admin, or to change what the site shows? Please email hello@sapu.earth and do not open a public issue. Say what you found and how to repeat it. We read every report and fix what is real as soon as we can.

Please test against a local copy (`sh scripts/dev.sh`), not against sapu.earth, and do not look at or change other people's data.
