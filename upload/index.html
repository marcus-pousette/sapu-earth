        SAFETY FIRST

        • Gloves and closed shoes for everyone. Bring drinking water and take breaks in the shade.
        • Nobody goes into water deeper than the knee or into fast water. Watch the tide and the waves. No work in heavy rain or thunder, or right after heavy rain.
        • Cover any cuts, and wash your hands with soap before you eat or drink. River and flood water can make people sick.
        • Leave needles, medical waste, chemicals and batteries alone. Say where they are in your message to Sapu Earth.
        • Only adults (18 or older) on the crew. Children stay away from the water and out of the photos and videos.
        • Bring the trash to a waste bank, a collector or the official collection point. Never burn it, and don't leave full sacks by the water.
        • If anything feels unsafe, stop.

        Put gloves, sacks, drinking water, a small first aid kit and transport in OTHER COSTS, under PRICE.

        How we handle your photos, videos and details
        • Who: Sapu Earth is run by Marcus Pousette in Innsbruck, Austria. Contact: hello@sapu.earth.
        • What you send: photos and videos (they may show people), the date, the place and the country, the exact location if you tap Use my location now, the numbers (kg, sacks, crew size, hours, costs and pay), where the trash went, your notes, the link to your post about the cleanup on Instagram or TikTok if you add one, and your message to Sapu Earth. For a mission also your plan, the day and the price. And your crew's logo, if you add one.
        • What is public: once we approve a cleanup, it is a public receipt on sapu.earth with everything you sent, except your message to Sapu Earth and the exact location. The location is shown rounded to about 100 m. The receipt also shows your crew's name, website and page, and what Sapu Earth paid. A mission is public in the same way once we publish or fund it. It is also in the list of missions at sapu.earth/missions, and once it is cleaned it stays there with its receipt, also after we stop working together. Once we approve your crew's logo, it shows next to your crew's name on sapu.earth. If you add the link to your post with a cleanup, its receipt can link to it, so people can comment there, unless we link our own post about the cleanup instead. Visitors can thank your crew with one tap under each receipt. The number of thanks shows on the receipt, on your crew's page and in the list of crews. Nobody signs in to do that, and we keep nothing about who tapped, so the counts cannot show who thanked. Your crew is also in the list of crews at sapu.earth/crews from the day we start working together, with its name, logo and country. Its name there links to your crew's public page on sapu.earth, which also shows your crew's website. Once your crew has a receipt or a mission on sapu.earth, the list also shows the totals of its receipts (cleanups, kilograms, sacks and what Sapu Earth paid), its newest receipt and its open and funded missions. It leaves the list when we stop working together.
        • Marketing: we also use your photos and videos, edited or as they are, to promote Sapu Earth, for example in posts, videos and ads on social media and other websites, and in print. We may name your crew and the place with them. Social networks also process what we post there, under their own rules.
        • What stays private: your message to Sapu Earth, the exact location, and everything we have not approved or published. Only the Sapu Earth team sees them.
        • On your phone: photos and your crew's logo are saved again as JPEG on your phone. This removes the camera data, including the location, before they are sent. The page also tries to remove the location and the phone brand and model from MP4 and MOV videos. WebM videos are sent as they are. Until you send, what you typed and picked stays on this phone, so nothing is lost if the page reloads. After a successful send it is deleted from the phone. The phone also remembers your language and whether you saved this link.
        • Why: to check your cleanups and missions, to decide what we fund, to pay your crew, to show your work on sapu.earth so anyone can see where the money goes, and to promote Sapu Earth. Legal basis: our agreement to fund your crew's cleanups (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)), the consent of everyone shown in the photos and videos (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)), and our legitimate interest in public receipts and in making Sapu Earth known (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). Without the photos and the numbers we cannot fund or show a cleanup.
        • Where: on Cloudflare servers (database and file storage), which may be outside the EU. Cloudflare stores them for us as our processor. It is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and uses the EU standard contractual clauses for other countries. This page loads all its files, fonts included, from sapu.earth.
        • How long: photos and videos you upload but never send are deleted after 48 hours. Approved cleanups stay on sapu.earth as public receipts, also after we stop working together. If we don't approve a cleanup, or take it off the site later, we delete its photos and videos. A mission nobody funds is removed after 28 days waiting for us, or after 28 days on sapu.earth. When a mission is removed for any reason, for example because you withdraw it, its photos and video, your message and its exact location are deleted. 90 days after we decide on a cleanup, or after a mission is done, we delete your message to Sapu Earth and round the exact location to about 100 m. Your crew's logo is deleted when we do not use it, when it waits 90 days without our check, when a newer one replaces it, and when we stop working together. The link to your post is deleted when we take it off the receipt, with its cleanup when we do not approve it or take it off the site, and when we stop working together. The thanks stay with their receipt, but we may take off the thanks of a day that looks inflated. We keep the rest, such as places, dates and numbers, as our record for as long as Sapu Earth runs.
        • Your rights: ask to see, correct or delete your details, withdraw a consent, or object to how we use them, by emailing hello@sapu.earth. Anyone shown in a photo or video can ask us the same way to take it down. We then stop using it and remove it where we can. You can also complain to the Austrian data protection authority (dsb.gv.at).
