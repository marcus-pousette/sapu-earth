BECOME A PARTNER CREW

Do you clean beaches, rivers or streets with a team? Sapu Earth pays local crews for cleanups and shows every one on a public map. Tell us about your crew. It takes about 3 minutes.

There are no fees, and we never ask for a PIN, a password or an OTP code.

HOW IT WORKS

  1. You post a dirty spot as a mission, with your own price in local money.
  2. We check it. Only funded missions are paid, so wait for our OK before you start.
  3. We send an advance to one person's own bank account or e-wallet, and the rest after we approve your cleanup.

Already a crew with us? You don't need to apply again: open the private link from our WhatsApp or email message. Lost it? Write to hello@sapu.earth and we send you a new one.

We fund a few crews at a time, so we can't say yes to everyone. We reply to every application on WhatsApp or email, usually within a week. No calls needed.

How we handle your details
  • Who: Sapu Earth is run by Marcus Pousette in Innsbruck, Austria. Contact: hello@sapu.earth.
  • What we store: your answers, your contact details, up to 3 photos, your crew's logo if you add one, and for about a day a scrambled code made from your internet address, to stop spam. Until you send, the answers you type stay on this phone as a draft. After sending, the phone keeps only your language choice.
  • Why: to review your application and reply to you, and if we work together, to show your crew on sapu.earth from the first day: its name, country and logo in the list of crews at sapu.earth/crews, and on its public page also one of the links you gave us. Legal basis: your consent and steps before working together (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a) and (b)).
  • Where: on Cloudflare servers (database and file storage), which may be outside the EU. Cloudflare stores them for us as our processor. It is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and uses the EU standard contractual clauses for other countries. This page loads all its files, fonts included, from sapu.earth.
  • How long: photos are deleted once we decide, and so is your crew's logo, unless we work together and show it on sapu.earth: then it stays there until a newer one replaces it or we stop working together. Applications we don't take forward are deleted, and so is any application we haven't decided on within 3 months. If we work together, we keep your contact details while we do and delete them when we stop.
  • Your rights: ask to see, correct or delete your details, or withdraw your consent, by emailing hello@sapu.earth. You can also complain to the Austrian data protection authority (dsb.gv.at).
