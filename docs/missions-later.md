# Missions: the full design, for later

Not for now. This is the complete design from November 2026 (trust levels, caps, ranks, bounties, sponsors, donations). Version 1 is the much smaller [missions.md](missions.md); take ideas from here only when a real problem asks for them.

## 1. What it is

A crew posts a mission: a photo of a dirty spot, the job and a budget. You fund it, the crew cleans, and a checked receipt releases the rest of the money. Each mission is a story (now, funded, after) at a short link like sapu.earth/m/12.

## 2. The flow (phase 2)

1. **Post.** The crew adds a spot (do waste pickers work there?) and 1 to 4 "now" photos from the page camera, one showing this week's paper code (like K7QX). Then they add the job, crew size, a day 3 or more days ahead, the budget in local money with pay per person, and tick: all paid are 18+, no children in photos, OK to publish.
2. **Review (you, 3 min).** New spots get map and older imagery links. PUBLISH AND FUND, or REJECT with a ready reply in the crew's language.
3. **Fund.** The 30% advance goes by Wise that day, with a cleanup code that works offline.
4. **Clean.** Same-angle before and after photos (one of each with the code), a 15 second walking video, kg or sacks, a disposal photo.
5. **Verify (you, 3 min, within 48 hours).** The previous "after", the "now", the new "before" and "after" side by side, with red flags. APPROVE publishes /r/<id> and the reveal on /m/12; you send the 70%.
6. **Crew paid.** The lead pays each member within a day and uploads the screenshots (names cropped), or gets no new funding.

About 10 minutes per mission with both transfers, plus your own reveal post ("mission 12, link in bio").

## 3. States and pins

| State | Globe |
|---|---|
| review | hidden; /m/ answers 404 |
| open | orange hollow ring, pulsing, "NEXT UP": now photo, budget, days left |
| funded | yellow dot: funder, day (never a time) |
| done | solid green dot, opens the receipt |
| removed | expired (28 days unfunded), cancelled, rejected or unpublished: gone, photos deleted |

Only OPEN pulses; today's orange receipt pins turn green. No receipt 14 days after the day: OVERDUE in /admin/.

Stamps follow the proof: VERIFIED (camera and codes, checked by you or a verifier), REVIEWED (gallery fallback, today's receipts), ORG REPORT (org-checked).

## 4. Trust levels

| | Org | Vetted crew | Individual (phase 4) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Who | orgs and companies with paid crews (like Pro Ocean) | small paid crews | one adult near home |
| Joins | your invite, rules agreed by email | /apply/ | in person, via the coordinator |
| Cap | EUR 250, you raise it after 5 clean missions | Rookie EUR 80; Pro 120 at 3 verified; Captain 150 at 10 | bounty EUR 5; 10 at 5 verified; 15 at 15 |
| At once | 3 open, 2 funded | Rookie 1 open, 1 funded; then 2 and 2 | 1 claim, 3 paid a week |
| Payout | 100% after, on invoice | 30% at FUND, 70% at APPROVE | 100% after verification, same day |
| Proof | same camera flow and codes | camera, codes, video, disposal photo, pay screenshots | the same, before and after within 3 h and 50 m |
| Verifies | the org; you check completeness; spot check 1 in 10 | you; spot check 1 in 4 | peer verifier on 1 in 3 (EUR 1 to 2 each); coordinator visits 1 in 5 |

Payees need a verified wallet (DANA Premium, GoPay Plus, verified GCash); basic Indonesian ones cap at Rp 2 million (EUR 105). Rank on /p/<slug> counts verified missions only; checks, strikes and cap cuts stay private, so nobody learns which mission was checked. Without a checker in the area, crews stay Rookie. Honest mistakes get 48 hours; staging ends the partnership unpaid, without naming anyone.

Ogyre and Sungai Watch get the org invite too. If they prefer their own model (Ogyre sells per kg, Sungai Watch runs its own cleanup days), fund them directly as agreed by email and publish their reports as ORG REPORT receipts, outside missions.

## 5. Money

- **Funders.** You now; companies from phase 3 (sponsorship: invoiced business income); the public via Open Collective Europe (OCE) in phase 5. One funder per mission until then.
- **Payouts.** Wise EUR to the lead's wallet; leads invoice monthly as freelancers. Crews know before the day that pay comes within about 3 days. In Indonesia never wire to an organization (Ormas rules).
- **Fees.** Wise has a fixed part per transfer: about 1 to 2% for a mission's two, shown on the receipt. Bounties come from the coordinator's weekly float, check fees monthly.
- **Per mission, never per kg,** which invites wet sand and undercuts waste pickers.
- **At risk.** A vanished crew costs its advance (at most EUR 45). FUND refuses past EUR 150 of advances without a verified receipt, or past the monthly cap.
- **Records.** The MONEY tab logs every transfer (Wise reference, invoice) for a monthly CSV to your accountant.

## 6. Anti-fraud

Biggest risk: staging (dumping trash to get paid to remove it), then old or borrowed photos.

- **Spots, not piles.** River mouths, beaches and canals refill on their own; a street or drain is easy to fill with one dumped bag, so "clear my street" is out for now. You confirm each new spot once. One paid mission per spot every 14 days, 7 at an org's trash barrier (it refills weekly).
- **"Now" is the baseline.** A much bigger pile on the day, or fresh intact bags, signals staging.
- **Fresh proof.** Paper codes prove photos are newer than the post and the funding; GPS, time and fingerprints catch reuse. If the camera fails, gallery photos pass as REVIEWED, without rank.
- **Red flags:** over 150 m from the spot; before and after over 12 h apart or before funding; a reused photo; no video; spot too soon; over the cap; pay under the floor.
- **Spot checks.** Announced, secret timing. A checker from another network gets EUR 5 to 10 whatever they find, and checks the site, crew size, 18+ and waste pickers.

**Phase 4, bounties in one neighbourhood.** The coordinator signs people up in person (upload link, 18+ check, gloves and sacks, a signed no-insurance note). Anyone reports spots on /spot/ for "spotted by" credit, never cash (that would pay people to dump); the coordinator confirms each on site. First come, first claimed, for 48 hours, never by the reporter (person, phone or wallet). Only the claim holder is paid, the same day, from the float; the spot then rests 7 days. Peer verifiers are never local, paid the same either way, and get known fakes mixed in.

## 7. Safety and ethics

- Everyone paid is 18+ and earns at least the pay floor; photos with children are rejected.
- Camera screens (EN/ID): gloves, closed shoes; no traffic, deep or fast water, storms. Needles, medical or chemical waste, batteries: photograph, leave, report; the mission still pays.
- **Disposal.** No burning or dumping. Residual waste goes to a TPS, TPS3R or barangay MRF (drop-off photo with the code); recyclables to a bank sampah or junkshop (slip if weighed) or to waste pickers.
- **Waste pickers (pemulung).** Where they work, no mission unless they join the paid crew, keeping what they sort.
- A courtesy note to the RT/RW or barangay head. Faces only with consent; exact GPS only in /admin/.
- An EN/ID privacy notice on the upload page covers GPS, slips, screenshots and retention.

## 8. What changes on the site

- **Upload page:** POST A MISSION; SEND A CLEANUP (crews pick a funded mission); MY MISSIONS (state, codes, share link, payments, rank).
- **/admin/:** MISSIONS tab (PUBLISH, PUBLISH AND FUND, CONFIRM SPOT, REJECT, CANCEL, CHECK RESULT, EN/ID messages); MONEY tab (to pay, at risk, month against cap, CSV).
- **Globe:** three pin kinds with a legend, a mission card, filter chips.
- **/m/12:** a number you can say on TikTok (captions carry no links). Now photo and plan, then funder and day, then a before and after slider. SHARE now, SPONSOR THIS SPOT (phase 3), FUND THIS (phase 5). "Today only Sapu Earth funds missions. Never send money to anyone for a mission."

## 10. Decisions (default in bold; * differs from what we agreed)

1. Caps, ranks, bounty steps, limits and check rates (section 4); a hard cap of EUR 400 committed a month. **Yes.**
2. Crew paid within 3 days; nobody fronts wages. **Yes.** Option: Captains get 60% at FUND and pay on the day.
3. \*PUBLISH AND FUND in one click; OPEN says NEXT UP, not NEEDS FUNDING. **Yes.**
4. Timeouts: 28 days unfunded; a funded mission is cancelled 30 days after its day (advance written off, back to Rookie). **Yes.**
5. Pay floor per person per day: Rp 150,000 or PHP 600. **Yes, checked with the first crews.**
6. Spot types (section 6); after phase 2, crews are paid only through missions. **Yes.**
7. \*Gallery fallback (section 6); you confirm spots; peer verifiers from phase 4; the drop-off photo is the disposal proof, a slip a bonus. **Yes.**
8. Verified wallets only; an Indonesian org without an entity abroad is paid through its named lead as a freelancer, or not at all. **Yes.**
9. Retention: after 12 months exact GPS and times are rounded, slips and screenshots deleted; removed missions after 90 days; payouts kept 7 years. **Yes.**
10. /m/ and pins in EN and ID; budgets in local money, EUR beside. **Yes.**
11. Accountant: sponsor price (budget plus 15%), VAT and Kleinunternehmer, crew spending as an expense, whether later gifts turn taxable. **Ask before phase 3.**
12. \*No gifts before OCE, not even small direct ones (sponsor invoices tie Sapu Earth to your business); FUND THIS from phase 5. **Yes.**

## Appendix: technical (short)

**Migration `0004_missions.sql`** only adds things, so it runs before the code (like 0003).

- `partners` + `trust` (org, crew or individual; crew by default), `verified_count`, `checks_passed`, `strikes`, `cap_eur`, `lang`, `contact_whatsapp`, `contact_email`, `post_code`, `post_code_at`. Approving an application copies lang and contact; NEW PARTNER takes them typed; DEACTIVATE wipes them like the application contact. The rank is computed from `verified_count`.
- `spots`: `id`, `partner_id`, `name`, `kind` (river_mouth, beach, canal), `country`, `lat`, `lng` (exact, private), `pemulung`, `status` (new, confirmed or retired), `confirmed_at`, `confirm_note`, `min_days`, `created_at`.
- `missions`: `id`, `no` UNIQUE (the short public number), `client_ref` UNIQUE, `partner_id`, `doer_id` (the poster until phase 4), `spot_id`, `status` (review, open, funded, done, removed), `removed_why`, `task`, `plan_date`, `crew_size`, `currency`, `budget_local`, `crew_pay_local`, `budget_eur`, `advance_eur`, `funder`, `proof` (verified, reviewed or org), `adults_ok`, `consent`, `post_code`, `day_code`, `private_note`, `review_note`, `check_result`, `check_note`, `crew_paid_at`, `created_at`, `published_at`, `funded_at`, `closed_at`, `submission_id`.
- `uploads` + `mission_id`, `role` (NULL, now, slip or pay), `taken_at`, `lat`, `lng`, `acc_m`, `from_camera` (a hint only), `dhash`. `submissions` + `mission_id`.
- `payouts`: `id`, `partner_id` (NULL for checkers), `payee_label`, `mission_id`, `kind` (advance, final, float, bounty, check or other; bounties paid from the float are logged too), `eur`, `fee_eur`, `local_amount`, `currency`, `wise_ref`, `invoice_ref`, `paid_at`.
- Indexes on the status, partner, spot and mission_id lookups, and on `payouts.paid_at`.

**Endpoints**

- `POST /api/mission?k=`, built like `submit.js`, with the same consent check plus `adults_ok`. It reuses a spot within 150 m or creates one, attaches the "now" uploads, and checks the limits, the cap (with a rough rate in code) and the pay floor. `plan_date` needs its own validator (at least 3 days ahead), because `isoDate()` rejects future dates.
- `GET /api/missions`: open and funded missions, public fields, rounded coordinates, cached for 60 s. It stays separate from `/api/events`, because `site/app.js` (and old cached copies of it) treat that array as receipts. Expiry runs in `waitUntil`, at most 50 rows, like `expireApplications`.
- `GET /m/<no>`: like `r/[id].js`, with Open Graph tags for each state (shared links carry `?funded` or `?done`, because WhatsApp and Facebook keep the first preview of a URL), then `/#m-<no>`. It answers 404 in review. `_routes.json` already routes `/m/*` to Functions.
- Admin: `GET /api/admin/missions` (flags computed on load, like `possible_duplicates`), `POST /api/admin/missions/decide` (publish, publish_fund, fund, confirm_spot, reject, cancel, check; funding refuses past the advance ceiling or the monthly cap), `GET|POST /api/admin/payouts` (with CSV) and `POST /api/admin/partners/trust`.
- Changed: `GET /api/partner` adds missions, codes, rank and limits. `POST /api/upload` accepts `kind=now` (stored as `before`) and `kind=slip` or `kind=pay` (stored as `after`), with `role` set, plus the capture fields as query params. The `kind` CHECK stays, so no table rebuild is needed. `POST /api/submit` requires a funded `mission_id` unless the partner is an org, requires one video for missions, and counts slips and screenshots apart from the after photos.

**Reuse**

- **Upload flow.** "Unattached" becomes `submission_id IS NULL AND mission_id IS NULL` in `cleanOrphans`, the 30-file quota and `submit.js`; otherwise mission photos would be deleted after 48 hours. Photos use `capture="environment"` and the Geolocation API at each shot; `file.lastModified` only sets the `from_camera` hint. The dHash comes from the canvas that already re-encodes the photo. Video uses `MediaRecorder` at about 480p and stops at 15 s: a few MB of WebM with no location inside.
- **Media.** "Now" photos stay in the private bucket while the mission is open or funded, and `media/[key].js` serves them with a 1 hour cache. They are copied to `PUBLIC_MEDIA` only at APPROVE, with the rest. The copy loop in `decide.js` skips `role IN ('slip', 'pay')` (slips can show a waste-bank customer's name or account), `mediaFor` puts those in an admin-only group, and `media/[key].js` never serves them publicly.
- **Receipts.** `decide.js` approve raises `verified_count` in the same batch only on the funded to done transition (guarded by `status = 'funded'`), so approving again to fix amounts does not count twice. Rejecting a pending cleanup leaves the mission funded, so the crew can resend. Rejecting an approved one (UNPUBLISH) sets the mission to removed, deletes all its media including the now photos from both buckets, lowers `verified_count` and keeps the payout rows; purge the edge cache by hand as today. `toEvent()` gets `proof` and an optional `mission` block (now photo, funder, payments, fee), documented in `data/README.md`; `site/app.js` picks the stamp from `proof` instead of stamping every non-demo receipt VERIFIED, draws receipts as green dots without rings, and adds missions from `/api/missions` (pulsing orange ring for open, yellow for funded).
- **Privacy.** The EN/ID notice goes into `upload/index.html` and `upload/upload.js`, like the one on `/apply/`. A retention run, like `expireApplications`, rounds GPS and times and deletes slips and screenshots after 12 months, and deletes removed missions after 90 days; `payouts` rows stay 7 years.
- **Applications.** `createPartner()` is unchanged, and new partners start as `crew`. You set orgs to `org` after NEW PARTNER. COPY MESSAGE reuses the applications pattern with `partners.lang`.
- **Edge.** The one rate limiting rule already counts every path that runs a Function, `/m/` and a phase 3 `/s/` included. Phase 4's static `/spot/` and `/check/` go into `scripts/build-site.sh` (the copy loop, the perl list of index.html files and its folder pattern) and into the `_routes.json` excludes, and once that deploy is live, into the rule's exclusions (upload-flow.md, step 4, item 5).
- **Tests.** `tests/e2e.sh`: one mission end to end, limits, ceiling and monthly cap, expiry, 404 in review, now photos surviving the orphan cleanup, slips and screenshots never public, a second approve counting once, UNPUBLISH removing a done mission.

**Docs to update when phase 2 ships:** `ops/coordinator-agreement.md` (no event float for missions, crew paid within 3 days instead of the same day), `ops/provider-receipt-spec.md` (missions go through the upload link, email delivery stays for direct funding, rules agreed by email), `ops/verification.md` (ranks replace the +10% bonus for mission crews), `ops/event-checklist.md` (camera flow, codes, disposal photo), README principle 5, `docs/upload-flow.md` and `data/README.md`.

**Left out until it hurts:** notifications, a timer (expiry runs on page visits), a 9:16 card maker, live "on site now" pins (they would show where a crew is).

**Later, no redesign needed.** Phase 3 adds a `sponsors` table and `/s/<id>`. Phase 4 adds `spots.reported_by`, a `claims` table (mission, person, expires_at), verifier partners and `partners.wallet_hash`, an HMAC under a new `HASH_KEY` secret rather than `ADMIN_TOKEN`, named in the privacy notice.
