# Missions, version 1

Keep it simple. Bigger ideas (trust levels, caps, bounties, sponsors, donations) wait in [missions-later.md](missions-later.md) until a real problem asks for them.

## What it is

A crew posts a **mission**: photos of a dirty spot as it looks now and, if they like, a short video of it, what they will do, the day, and their price. Marcus funds it or not. The crew cleans and sends the cleanup through their upload link as today. Marcus approves it, and the mission becomes a receipt on the globe.

## The flow

1. **Post.** On their crew page (the private upload link) the crew taps POST A MISSION: 1 to 4 photos of the spot now, if they like one short video of it (at most 90 seconds and 95 MB), place and location, what they will do, the day (a week ahead by default), and under PRICE their price in local money: the currency, how many people will work (it starts on their last mission's crew size), the pay for each person and any other costs. Crews never type a total: the page shows the crew's pay under the pay ("15 people × IDR 100,000 = IDR 1,500,000 for the crew") and the sum in a box, and sends both totals (the price and the crew's pay inside it). The task of their last funded mission at the same place, or else of their last funded mission, is one tap away, and so are its pay for each person and other costs, but only while that mission's crew pay splits evenly (never a price posted as one total). A SAFETY FIRST box on the form lists the basics (gloves, closed shoes, drinking water, no water above the knee or fast water, cover cuts, no needles or chemicals, adults only, never burn the trash, stop if unsafe), and the welcome message with their link already explains the steps and the money. They tick that the photos and videos may be published, everyone paid is 18 or older, and no children are in them.
2. **Review.** In /admin/, MISSIONS tab, Marcus sees the photos and the video, the map link and the price. He can:
   - **FUND**: type the EUR amount he agrees to and the advance (30% by default). The mission shows on the globe as funded, and the admin says how much to send now with Wise. Then a ready "it is funded" message (EN / ID) opens on the card, to send after the advance: the amounts and where the money goes (the crew's bank account or e-wallet, in local money at the exchange rate of the day it is sent; like the welcome message, it names no payment service), the day, the #12 paper, the safety basics and how to send the cleanup.
   - **PUBLISH**: show it on the globe as open ("NEXT UP") without funding it yet.
   - **DECLINE**: a ready reply in the crew's language to copy, then the mission is closed: its photos and video are deleted, its private note and exact location are wiped.
3. **Clean.** The funded mission's card on the crew page says so, with the day and the paper with the mission number (like #12) to hold up in one before photo. On the day, SEND A CLEANUP FOR #12 on the card opens the cleanup with the mission's place, country and crew size already in, and the paper right above the before photos; the costs stay folded, since the receipt shows the price the crew asked. "No mission" stays possible: under CHANGE in the form, and as the plain link SEND A CLEANUP WITHOUT A MISSION on the crew page.
4. **Approve.** The cleanup appears in PENDING next to the mission's "now" photos and video and the agreed amount. APPROVE publishes the receipt and marks the mission done. Marcus sends the rest of the money. If the crew sent the cleanup with "No mission", PENDING offers "Link to mission" with the crew's funded missions, so APPROVE can still close the right one.

## Price

Crews ask whatever they want. Marcus sees the price before funding and can decline or reply with a counter-offer. The price is the whole mission: the crew size × the pay for each person (the same for everyone), plus the other costs, added up by the page, so the crew's pay is never on top of the price. Extra pay for a leader goes into the other costs. Two things keep it honest without rules:

- The admin shows **funded this month** against the monthly budget, so it is easy to see when the month is full.
- The crew's pay, per person and in all, is on the mission and on the public receipt.

A price posted before the page added it up (like mission #2) may hold a crew pay that does not split evenly: it shows as one amount with its head count (CREW PAY IDR 100,000 FOR 15 PPL), never as a pay for each person, gets no price chip, and /admin/ marks it in orange.

## On the site

- **Globe:** three kinds of pins: open missions (yellow tags with a trash bag), funded missions (black tags with a broom), and receipts (safety green dots on a white rim, done), with a small legend that reads OPEN, FUNDED, CLEANED, the steps a mission goes through. Missions whose tags would touch on screen share one tag ("#1 #2", or "#1 +3" when there are more than 3). Zooming in splits a tag once its missions are far enough apart on screen, but even the deepest zoom shows about 2 to 3 km per screen pixel, so missions less than about 100 km apart keep one tag. Near the rim of the moving globe two tags can still come close. Then the one with a funded mission (or else the one first in NEXT UP) stays, and the other fades out until it has room again. Tapping a tag opens its mission, or lists them when the tag stands for several. Tags and the zoom buttons take taps on at least 44 by 44 px, and a finger tap within about 36 px of a receipt dot opens that receipt (or a short list to pick from, when dots sit too close together for a finger). Zoom with + and -, Ctrl + scroll (⌘ + scroll on a Mac), a trackpad pinch or a double click. A plain scroll still scrolls the page, and the globe shows how to zoom for a moment. On a phone, pinch. Tags never cover the zoom buttons.
- **Homepage:** a NEXT UP strip above the receipts: funded missions from today on first (soonest first), then open ones, then the ones whose day has passed. A card with a video of the spot shows ▶ VIDEO, and the mission post plays it (NOW · VIDEO). The mission post links the crew's name to its page (`/p/<slug>`), with the crew's logo next to it once Marcus approved one (docs/upload-flow.md, Crew logos). SEE THE CREWS stands next to SEE THE RECEIPTS on the first screen, always, also next to the samples (docs/upload-flow.md, Homepage: the ways on). NEXT UP's head links ALL MISSIONS while its cards are real and the footer THE MISSIONS once sapu.earth/missions lists something, and a real mission's post ends with ALL MISSIONS. On a wide screen the mission post shows every photo of the spot and its video large next to the paper, one at a time (docs/upload-flow.md, The post on a wide screen).
- **Receipts:** a receipt that closed a mission shows the mission number, photos and video, and what the crew asked in local money (ASKED, CREW PAY as 15 × IDR 100,000, OTHER COSTS) when the cleanup came without costs of its own, else what it cost (SPENT, CREW PAY, OTHER COSTS). In the receipt post, CLEANED BY links the crew's page and shows its logo while the crew is active; the cards keep the name alone. On a wide screen the receipt post opens on its first before and after side by side and shows every photo and video of the cleanup and of the mission it closed (docs/upload-flow.md, The post on a wide screen).
- **The list of crews:** sapu.earth/crews lists every active crew: on top those with a receipt or an open or funded mission on sapu.earth, with up to 3 of their open and funded missions (funded first, then the soonest day), each linking its share page /m/<no>, and under JUST JOINED · FIRST MISSION COMING the others, with their name, logo and country only, until they have a published or funded mission or an approved cleanup; a crew whose mission came and went (expired unfunded, withdrawn or cancelled) stays on top as "no cleanups yet" (docs/upload-flow.md, The list of crews).
- **The list of missions:** sapu.earth/missions lists the open, funded and cleaned missions in that order, with chips once two of them have missions; an open or funded one links its post, a cleaned one its receipt (docs/upload-flow.md, The list of missions).
- **Crew page:** /p/<slug> shows NEXT UP between the crew's numbers and its receipts: its open and funded missions with the first photo of the spot, the day and the crew size, each linking /m/<no>. ALL CREWS at the foot links /crews. ALL MISSIONS ON SAPU.EARTH under NEXT UP links /missions.
- **Share page:** every mission has a short link like sapu.earth/m/12 with its photo, and, for an MP4 clip while the mission is open or funded, its video (og:video) for apps that play one, made for TikTok ("mission 12, link in bio"). While open, its preview says what the crew asks as it adds up ("Asking IDR 3,000,000 in all: IDR 100,000 for each person and IDR 1,500,000 for other costs."). It follows the mission from open to funded to done. Once a mission is no longer on the map (expired, declined, cancelled and so on), the link shows a small page that says so, with a link to the globe and one to the list of missions.
- **Upload page (the crew page):** the crew's missions as cards with their status, the next step as the one orange button (SEND A CLEANUP FOR #12 on a funded mission, else CONTINUE on unsent work, else POST A MISSION; none while a mission or a sent cleanup waits for Sapu Earth, since the next step is then to wait, and none at 5 missions waiting), SHARE and WITHDRAW, unsent work with CONTINUE, POST A MISSION, and the past missions. The status words stay honest: "Waiting for Sapu Earth", "On sapu.earth, not funded yet", "Funded: EUR x. Advance of EUR y on its way to you." The cards of missions in review, open and funded show "The price you asked" as it adds up, and the screen after POST MISSION says the price again, so the crew can catch a mistake before Sapu Earth funds it.

## Spots

One mission can run at up to 4 spots, each with its own place, point and day (Marcus said 'Yes, build it' on 2026-11-17). It stays one mission: one price, one FUND, one advance, one cleanup, one APPROVE and one receipt, and the stats count it once.

- **Where it is stored** (migration 0008, `functions/_lib/spots.js`): spot 1 is the mission's own place, point and day, as for every mission. Spots 2 to 4 are a list on the mission (`missions.spots`). The cleanup copies that list with the day the crew cleaned each spot (`submissions.spots`), and each before and after photo knows its spot (`uploads.spot`). The points of spots 2 to 4 are kept at the public precision (3 decimals, about 100 m), so they hold nothing private, and removing a mission or the 90-day trim leave them as they are.
- **Who adds a spot**: Sapu Earth, for now with an UPDATE in the D1 console (below). Crews cannot add spots on POST A MISSION yet; that comes when a crew needs it.
- **Globe and satellite map**: before the cleanup every spot is a pin. Spots a few km apart share the mission's tag (#12, once, its tip lists each spot); the satellite map has a marker at each spot. After the cleanup each spot is a green dot of its own, and every dot opens the one receipt.
- **Mission post, /m/<no> and /missions**: SPOT 1 and SPOT 2 rows with each place and day instead of LOC and DAY, one SEE SPOT 2 ON SATELLITE button for each spot, every place and day in the share preview ("A crew of 15 cleans Pantai Kuta on 5 Jan 2027 and Pantai Legian on 6 Jan 2027.") and on the /missions card ("days 5 and 6 Jan 2027"). The crew's page /p/<slug> and /crews still show spot 1 only.
- **Crew page**: the funded mission's card lists each spot with its day ("Spot 2: Pantai Legian (Wed 6 Jan)") and asks for the paper with #12 at each spot. SEND A CLEANUP FOR #12 asks 1 to 4 before and 1 to 4 after photos for each spot, each field named by its spot ("Spot 2: Pantai Legian", "Titik 2: Pantai Legian"), and the day of each spot (spot 1's is the cleanup date; the others start on their planned day once it has come). The videos (up to 30 before and 30 after, 60 in all, since 2026-11-19), RESULT (kg, sacks, crew, hours) and the costs stay for the whole mission, not per spot. Use my location now is not shown: the points are the mission's. The crew sends once, after the last spot.
- **Receipt**: one receipt, with SPOT 1 and SPOT 2 rows (place, country, day) instead of LOC and DATE. Its photos come spot by spot: on a phone the first before and the first after photo of each spot (BEFORE · SPOT 2), on a wide screen every photo, tagged BEFORE 1/2 · SPOT 2.
- **/admin/**: the mission card says "2 spots, days ... to ..." and has a Spot row for each (place, day, map link). PENDING groups the photos by spot (BEFORE, SPOT 2) and lists each spot with its day. LATE and OVERDUE count from the last spot's day, and so does the housekeeping's late count. The funded message (MESSAGE on the card) names each spot with its day and asks for the paper and the photos at each spot. A cleanup sent without a mission cannot be linked to a mission with spots: it has no photos for the other spots.
- **Money**: unchanged.

### Adding a spot (D1 console)

Run migration 0008 first (`sh scripts/remote-migrate.sh --apply`, docs/upload-flow.md, Spots). Then, in the Cloudflare dashboard, Storage & Databases, D1, **sapu-earth**, Console, one UPDATE for the mission. Spot 2's place as the crew calls it, its point from a Google Maps pin (lat first, then lng; `round(..., 3)` keeps it at the public precision) and its day. Here for mission #12, with what the crew sends:

```sql
UPDATE missions
SET spots = json_array(json_object('place_name', 'PLACE OF SPOT 2', 'lat', round(-8.000000, 3), 'lng', round(115.000000, 3), 'date', '2027-01-06'))
WHERE no = 12 AND status = 'funded' AND spots IS NULL;
SELECT no, place_name, plan_date, spots FROM missions WHERE no = 12;
```

It changes exactly one row (`changes: 1`); 0 means the mission is not funded any more or already has spots. A third spot is one more `json_object(...)` inside `json_array(...)`, in spot order. If spot 1's day moved too, `plan_date` is that day (`SET plan_date = '2027-01-05', spots = ...`). The crew page, the globe and /admin/ show the spots after a reload (`/api/missions` is cached for 60 seconds). To take the spots off again while no cleanup was sent: `UPDATE missions SET spots = NULL WHERE no = 12 AND status = 'funded';`.

## Housekeeping

- A mission that is not funded within 28 days disappears, with its photos and video.
- A funded mission with no cleanup 3 days after its day (the last day of its spots) is flagged LATE in /admin/, and the GitHub note emails Marcus about it. After 14 days it is flagged OVERDUE. Marcus can cancel it.
- A crew can have at most 5 missions waiting, on sapu.earth or funded at once.
- DEACTIVATE of a crew removes its missions in review, open and funded (the advance of a funded one stays with the crew). Done missions stay, they are receipts.
- When a mission is removed for any reason (declined, cancelled, withdrawn, expired, deactivated, unpublished), its photos and video are deleted and its private note and exact location are wiped. The place, the task, the dates and the money stay as the record.
- While a mission is live, the exact location stays in /admin/; the public map is rounded to about 100 m.
